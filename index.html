What we do

Every small shop has a thing somebody re-types. We fix that one thing.

We don't sell you a new system, a migration, or a platform. We build a small fix that sits next to what you already run, does the job, and stays out of the way. Sometimes that means building a small tool from scratch that fits how you already work.

  • The handoff. Numbers leave one screen and get typed into another. Every week. Every month.
  • The report. Built in a spreadsheet from numbers that already live in the system you already pay for.
  • The list that matters. Lives on a clipboard. Gets re-typed. Gets missed.

How it works

  1. Lunch. You tell us what eats your week. We listen and ask about the thing that gets re-typed.
  2. That evening. You get a working version to try on your own data. Not a proposal. Not a deck. The thing itself.
  3. If it helps. We set it up and stay on a monthly retainer, and it keeps getting better.

Nothing gets installed on an office computer. It runs where we can reach it, so when something changes we handle it without a site visit.

We price every job ourselves. We don't quote numbers on this page.
