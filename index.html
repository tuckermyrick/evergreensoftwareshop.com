What we do

Every district has a thing somebody re-types. Meter reads that go from the handheld into a spreadsheet and then into the billing program. The state report that gets built by hand every quarter. The shutoff list that lives on a clipboard.

We fix that one thing. Not a new system, not a migration, not a platform. A small fix that sits next to what you already run, does the job, and stays out of the way.

How it works

  • You tell us over lunch what eats your week.
  • That evening you get a working version to try on your own data.
  • If it helps, we set it up for the district and keep it running. A flat setup fee, then a monthly rate, and it keeps getting better.

Nothing gets installed on an office computer. It runs where we can reach it, so when something changes at the state or in your billing program, we handle it without a site visit.

Who we are

We're a small shop out of Spokane working with districts in the Spokane Valley and Kitsap County. We don't send a salesperson. The person you talk to is the person who does the work.
