What we do

Every district has a thing somebody re-types. We fix that one thing.

Not a new system, not a migration, not a platform. A small fix that sits next to what you already run, does the job, and stays out of the way.

  • Meter readsFrom the handheld into a spreadsheet and then into the billing program. By hand. Every month.
  • The state reportBuilt in a spreadsheet every quarter from numbers that already live in the billing system.
  • The shutoff listLives on a clipboard. Gets re-typed. Gets missed.

How it works

  1. Lunch.You tell us what eats your week. We listen and ask about the thing that gets re-typed.
  2. That evening.You get a working version to try on your own data. Not a proposal, not a deck. The thing itself.
  3. If it helps.We set it up for the district and keep it running. A flat setup fee, then a monthly rate, and it keeps getting better.

Nothing gets installed on an office computer. It runs where we can reach it, so when something changes at the state or in your billing program, we handle it without a site visit.
